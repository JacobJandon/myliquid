MyLiquid Gate, from an early-stage startup (no customers yet; SOC 2 audit planned, not done).
It sits in front of the MCP server or API your customers' AI agents use, as a proxy with no code change on your side. Before an agent's trade or payment executes, it checks who the agent is (AINRA agent passports today; other Know-Your-Agent standards planned), what the customer allowed and your caps. Anything above the agent's limits goes to a human for approval, and one call stops every agent.
Every decision is kept as tamper-evident evidence you can export under FINRA and MAS SAFR headings.
