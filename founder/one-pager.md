# MyLiquid Gate · one page

**Verdict: Not yet** (computed by the plan from the numbers: year 1 is a loss, and the panel's buy rate is under the
bar). This page is what it would take to change that.

**The problem.** Brokers and crypto venues opened their accounts to customers' AI agents this year. They cannot tell
which agent is acting or whether it stayed inside what the customer allowed, and when an examiner asks, they have no
record to show.

**The customer.** The compliance officer, head of product or CTO at a mid-size retail broker or crypto venue that
offers agent access (an MCP server or an agent API).

**The offer.** Gate decides whether an AI agent may make a trade or payment, and keeps the proof. Start with a
**Shadow Pilot**: Gate replays a mirrored copy of your agent traffic out of band and reports what it would have
allowed, sent to a person or refused, with an exam pack mapped to FINRA and MAS SAFR headings. **$1,500 for 60 days,
credited to year 1, refunded in full if the exam pack fails your compliance review.** Then Growth at **$1,500 a
month**, price fixed for 24 months. Five design-partner slots.

A "day" in this table is a month: one unit is one paying customer for one month, so break-even is 38 paying
customers. Each one leaves $1,426.20 a month (95%) after its own costs.

**The evidence so far.**
- Simulated buyer panel, 100 decision-makers: 4% would take the original free pilot; trust was the reason for 47 of
  the 96 who passed, price for 1. Re-test of the Shadow Pilot offer, 20 buyers: 10% would pay. Simulated buyers are
  a first read, not demand.
- Competitor gap: funded MCP gateways (Runlayer, Archestra) and agent-identity players (Skyfire, Vouched, the card
  networks) each cover part of it; none found in our search sells agent-identity-aware order and payment
  controls with supervisor-ready evidence to the mid-size brokers and venues opening MCP. Market size is not established yet.
- Real test: none yet. The first is below.

**What the money is for.** $670,249 on the full plan (two founders, an engineer, a BD lead, a fractional compliance
officer, insurance, a SOC 2 programme), or $378,817 if the engineer and BD lead are hired only against signed
customers. Nothing is raised before the test.

**The ask.** Introductions to compliance or product owners at brokers and crypto venues offering agent access, and,
for those firms, a Shadow Pilot slot. **The test: 3 paid pilots by 18 January 2027.** If it fails, the offer changes
before any money is spent.
