## What would flip a no

- A free or near-free real-money tier with a named, licensed broker holding my account, plus proof over a year of demo use that the agent actually beats my plain monthly index buys after the fee.
- Naming the actual licensed broker (one I recognize, SIPC-covered) along with a real monthly price.
- If it worked inside the broker account I already have and cost nothing extra, with a few months of demo results showing it actually did better than what I do by hand.
- If it plugged into the broker I already use and cost a couple of bucks a month, and after a few months of demo money it clearly did better than my own payday routine.
- If it cost the same as or less than what my robo-adviser takes now, with no separate subscription, and the broker partner was a name I already trust.
- A named, licensed broker with my account insured, plus a plain written answer on who pays if the agent makes a bad trade, and a few months on the free demo where the pet actually beat my own monthly investing.
- A named, licensed broker holding my account with real insurance, and a mode where the agent only suggests trades and never executes without my tap.
- If it were free or cost nothing beyond what my broker already charges, and it clearly did better than my plain monthly index fund buys.
- Knowing which licensed, SIPC-covered broker actually holds the money, plus a few months of the demo agent's record clearly beating what I do on my own.
- A named, regulated broker holding the account with SIPC coverage, plus a mode where the agent only suggests and nothing moves without my tap, with a public track record.
- If the real-money version were free or close to it at the base level, ran through a broker I already know, and the agent showed it could actually beat my plain monthly index buys after fees.
- A well-known, licensed broker named as the partner plus a pile of solid reviews from people and finance creators I follow who used it with real money for a while.
